The Story

A MODERN-DAY
BONNIE & CLYDE

Two small-time hustlers. One impossible conspiracy. Everything in Leonida — the sun, the salt air, the law — is stacked against them.

J

PROTAGONIST 01JASON DUVAL

Grew up around grifters and never really left the life. Army veteran — note the paratrooper tattoo — now drifting through the Leonida Keys, working for local drug runners and living rent-free at Brian Heder’s place. He wants the easy life. Meeting Lucia could be the best — or worst — thing that ever happened to him.

L

PROTAGONIST 02 · FIRST PLAYABLE FEMALE LEADLUCIA CAMINOS

From Liberty City to Leonida Penitentiary — “fighting for her family” put her inside, and sheer luck walked her out. Taught to fight by her father from a young age. All she wants is the good life her mom has dreamed of since Liberty City… but Lucia is prepared to take matters into her own hands.

“When an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America — in the middle of a conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida, forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.” — Rockstar Games · Official Launch Story
